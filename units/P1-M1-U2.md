## Objective

Wire one shared entry point for starting a future capture session.

## In scope

- Add a small `HotkeyManager` that registers `Cmd+Shift+X`.
- Introduce `CaptureSessionCoordinator` as the single place where capture sessions begin.
- Route both the status item capture action and the hotkey callback through that coordinator.

## Non-goals

- Actual screenshot capture.
- Permission prompting.
- Overlay windows or selection behavior.

## Completion contract

- Pressing the configured hotkey and choosing the menu action hit the same coordinator path.
- The app stays buildable and stable even though later milestones still fill in the actual capture work.
- The hotkey remains fixed to `Cmd+Shift+X` for V0.1.
