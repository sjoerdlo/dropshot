## Objective

Make the live scroll session produce a real final image safely.

## In scope

- Feed captured strips into the stitching engine during the live session.
- Trim the composite when the user scrolls slightly backward.
- Enforce the output size guardrail from the plan before returning the final image.

## Non-goals

- Preview UI.
- Extra smart auto-stop behavior.
- Horizontal or nested-scroll support.

## Completion contract

- Stopping a scroll session returns a final stitched image or a clear guardrail failure.
- Small reverse scroll does not ruin the output.
- The integration remains scoped to one session and one vertical composite.
