## Objective

Create the interactive control surface that remains clickable while the overlay passes events through.

## In scope

- Add a small floating HUD with `Done` and `Cancel`.
- Anchor the HUD near the selected region.
- Route HUD actions back into the coordinator through explicit callbacks or delegates.

## Non-goals

- Scroll monitoring.
- Stitching output.
- Preview or save UI.

## Completion contract

- The app can show and dismiss a floating `Done` and `Cancel` HUD tied to the current selection.
- HUD actions are plumbed but do not yet finalize stitched output.
- The HUD remains intentionally tiny and single-purpose.
