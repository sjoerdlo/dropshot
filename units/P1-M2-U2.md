## Objective

Show the captured screen back to the user as the fullscreen foundation for region selection.

## In scope

- Add the borderless overlay window controller for the captured screen.
- Present the frozen screenshot fullscreen above the chosen screen.
- Hand lifecycle control back to the coordinator for later selection and dismissal work.

## Non-goals

- Drag selection.
- Scroll pass-through mode.
- Done or Cancel HUD controls.

## Completion contract

- Triggering capture opens a fullscreen frozen overlay over the target screen.
- Overlay presentation and teardown are owned by the coordinator and controller, not mixed into the capture primitive.
- No selection or scroll behavior is introduced yet.
